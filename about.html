About Us

A dedicated academic resource platform for students pursuing the BCA programme under Dibrugarh University and the Centre for Distance and Open Education.

About Dibrugarh University

Dibrugarh University is a prominent institution of higher learning in Assam, recognized for its commitment to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic student development. With a strong focus on quality education and intellectual growth, it continues to serve as an important center for higher learning in the region.

About CDOE and the BCA Programme

The Centre for Distance and Open Education (CDOE) extends educational opportunities to learners seeking flexibility, access, and academic continuity. The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) programme is designed to build a strong foundation in programming, database systems, software development, web technologies, and information management, equipping students for evolving careers in the digital age.

Purpose of This Portal

This portal has been developed to provide BCA students with a centralized, student-friendly platform for academic support. It brings together notes, previous year question papers, syllabus information, and essential learning resources in one place to promote efficient study habits and better academic access.
